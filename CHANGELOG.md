# Changelog

All notable changes to this integration are documented in this file. The format
follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project
uses [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/).

Describe each change under `## [Unreleased]` as you make it. The Release
workflow moves that section under the version it ships, and the section becomes
the notes of the version's GitHub Release.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- An "Anime" calendar per user, filled from their public AniList list
  (watching, planning, or both): every airing from two weeks back to three
  months ahead, with its episode number, at the local time of the broadcast in
  Japan, linked to its AniList page.
- Titles in romaji, English or Japanese, the choice of each user.
- The calendar description says which anime are airing, which have no date
  yet, or why the list could not be read (unknown user, private list).
- Scene triggers "Episode today" (in the morning or at the air time) and "New
  episode announced".
- Scene action "Read the next episode": next episode, days left, status, last
  episode aired.
- Dashboard widget "Upcoming episodes".
- Administrator action "Check".
- English, French, German and Spanish.
